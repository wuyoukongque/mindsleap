On November 6, 2026, during the ninth China International Import Expo (CIIE), Lincoln Wang, Founder and CEO of MindsLeap and Partner at Founders Space, attended the 2026 International Innovation Cooperation Two-Way Empowerment Session and the inauguration ceremony of the Cross-Border E-Commerce Committee of the Yangtze River Delta G60 Science and Technology Innovation Corridor Enterprise Overseas Services Alliance. Held at the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai), the event provided a setting for international innovation exchange. MindsLeap and Founders Space also appeared at CIIE, showcasing their work connecting Silicon Valley's innovation ecosystem with Chinese enterprises and local industry use cases.

## A Window for Two-Way International Innovation

The session took place in the Innovation Incubation Area of the North Hall (NH), under the guidance of the Joint Conference Office of the Yangtze River Delta G60 Science and Technology Innovation Corridor. It was hosted by the corridor's Enterprise Overseas Services Alliance and organized by EGO International (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. The event focused on international innovation cooperation, business expansion overseas, and bringing innovative ventures into local industry settings.

For Chinese enterprises, international cooperation is not only about entering overseas markets. It also offers access to new technologies, business models, and ways of innovating. For overseas startup teams, China's industrial supply chains, use cases, and market needs offer opportunities to test technologies and explore commercialization. CIIE brought these complementary needs into the same setting, creating opportunities for further exchange and cooperation.

## Connecting Global Resources with Local Applications

The MindsLeap and Founders Space exhibition display presented startup acceleration, innovation exchange, enterprise AI transformation, and international cooperation as connected areas of service, highlighting the relationship between global innovation resources and Chinese industry needs.

MindsLeap is a global partner of Founders Space and an enterprise AI transformation and AI-native startup acceleration platform. Through entrepreneur exchanges, AI training and strategic advisory, FDE implementation, startup acceleration, and global growth services, it helps traditional enterprises explore AI applications and helps innovative ventures identify industry use cases and market opportunities. The value of these connections goes beyond introducing a technology or arranging a meeting. It lies in helping both sides understand practical questions: which business needs are worth solving, where a startup's technology fits, and what validation and implementation support a potential collaboration requires. Matching resources with specific needs is an important step toward sustained cooperation.

## Bringing Industry and Incubation Resources Together

The session included the committee's inauguration, the release of a service offering list, international cooperation agreements, incubation experience sharing, and technology venture pitches. By combining overseas business services with international innovation exchange, it provided different types of organizations with opportunities to connect.

Lincoln's participation also brought MindsLeap into a setting for exchange among international incubators, industrial parks, and overseas business service providers. For MindsLeap, enterprise AI transformation, startup acceleration, and international cooperation are interconnected: established enterprises need new technologies and organizational capabilities, startups need real industry problems to solve, and both benefit from more open resource networks.

## Connecting China and Silicon Valley with Real Business Needs

From exchanging frontier perspectives to enterprise practice, and from startup ventures to industrial applications, the lasting value of international innovation cooperation lies in sustained interaction among technology, talent, and markets.

The CIIE appearance provided an opportunity for MindsLeap and Founders Space to showcase their connection between global innovation ecosystems and Chinese industry. MindsLeap will continue to focus on enterprise AI transformation, AI-native entrepreneurship, and global cooperation, helping exchanges move from introductions toward a deeper understanding of needs. Its aim is to bring global innovation resources closer to the actual work of Chinese enterprises and help Chinese businesses explore new growth opportunities with a more open outlook.
